Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 8, 2026

This policy explains what information Where Is My Ghar processes, why it is used, which services handle it, and how you can request account deletion.

Publisher and contact

Where Is My Ghar is the publisher of the app and website. For privacy questions, support, or account-deletion requests, email supportwhereismyghar@gmail.com

Information we process and why

  • Account and profile information: sign-in primarily uses your mobile number, which maps to authentication credentials in the backend. Your name, mobile number, and other account details are processed for account access and account features.
  • Property listings and photos: listing details you submit are used to create owner listings and show them to people using the app. Uploading property photos is optional. If you upload photos, they are stored with the listing in Supabase to display property previews to guests.
  • Owner contact details: the owner name and phone number included in a listing support its contact options. If you tap a listing's call or WhatsApp button, the phone number is passed to your dialer; WhatsApp also receives a prefilled listing message.
  • Saved properties: saved-property and bookmark records support your saved listings feature.
  • Location services and navigation:
    • Owner View: when an owner chooses "Use my current location" while creating a listing, the app uses the device location to pin the property's precise location so guests can see it on the map.
    • Guest View: device location permission is required for the "Nearby First" property listing view. Guests can also view a property's location on its embedded map or choose "Navigate with Google Maps" to open Google Maps for directions.
  • Voice search: if you choose voice search, the browser/device Web Speech API converts speech to text for the search and may request microphone permission. This microphone permission is separate from location permission. Per the publisher, speech is processed on-device; Where Is My Ghar does not record, upload to its backend, or retain voice audio.
  • Device preferences: the web app stores preferences such as language and user role in browser storage on your device.

Permissions and your choices

  • You can deny or later revoke location permission in your browser or device settings. The "Nearby First" view and other location-based features may then be unavailable.
  • Voice search is optional and uses microphone permission, not location permission. You can use text search instead and manage microphone access through your browser or device settings.
  • Property photo uploads are optional. Photos you choose to upload are stored in Supabase with the listing for guest property previews.
  • Calling or messaging an owner is optional. If you choose a call or WhatsApp action, the selected phone app or service handles that interaction under its own privacy practices.
  • You can remove saved properties in the app or request deletion of your account and associated data using the process on our Account Deletion page.

Services that process information

  • Supabase provides account authentication and backend database/storage services for account profiles, property listings, saved properties, and uploaded listing photos.
  • OpenStreetMap Nominatim receives location coordinates for location-based city suggestions.
  • Google Maps receives location data when a map or navigation display is shown.
  • Unsplash provides fallback image assets used by the app.
  • Google Fonts supplies font assets requested by your browser when the site loads.
  • Browser/device Web Speech API processes voice-search input as described above.
  • WhatsApp and your device's phone app are opened only when you choose a support or listing call/WhatsApp action. For a listing contact, the owner's number is passed to the dialer for a call; WhatsApp receives the owner's number and a prefilled listing message. A support WhatsApp link opens with a prefilled support message.

These services process information under their own privacy notices and retention practices. Account deletion removes account data from Where Is My Ghar's Supabase backend; independent data processed by third-party services is handled under those services' own notices and retention practices.

Retention and account deletion

Active user profile and property listing data is retained while your account is active. For in-app deletion requests, the account enters an automated 30-day grace period. Signing back in during that period automatically cancels the request. When the 30 days expire, user profile data, property listings, uploaded photos, and saved bookmarks are automatically and permanently deleted from the backend.

If you cannot access the app, you can email supportwhereismyghar@gmail.com with your registered name and phone number. The support team manually reviews and processes email requests within 7 to 10 business days. This email process is separate from the in-app 30-day grace period.

Account deletion removes account data from Where Is My Ghar's Supabase backend. Independent data processed by third-party services is handled under their own privacy notices and retention practices. See the Account Deletion page for instructions.

Security

The publisher confirms that data is transmitted over HTTPS/TLS and backend data access is controlled using Supabase Row Level Security (RLS) policies. No additional security guarantees are made in this policy.

Policy updates

This policy may be updated from time to time. When it changes, the effective date at the top of this page will be revised. Please review this page periodically.

Contact

For privacy, support, or account-deletion requests, contact supportwhereismyghar@gmail.com
