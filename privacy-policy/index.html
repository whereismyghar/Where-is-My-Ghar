Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 23, 2026

This policy covers the Where Is My Ghar website and supported apps, including Android and iOS. It explains what information is used to help you find or list a home, how device permissions and storage work, which outside services are involved, and how to request account deletion.

Publisher and contact

Where Is My Ghar is the publisher of the website and apps on supported platforms, including Android and iOS. For privacy questions, support, or account-deletion requests, contact us by email

supportwhereismyghar@gmail.com

Information we process and why

  • Account and profile: you sign in with your mobile number and password. Your name and phone number support your account and, when included in a listing, help people contact you. Supabase manages your sign-in session, which is kept on your device so you can stay signed in.
  • Owners—property listings and photos: owners can submit property type and options, rent and deposit, address and location, facilities, availability, contact details, and photos. These details are stored with the listing in Supabase. Photo uploads are optional.

    Tenants browsing while signed out: you can see public details such as city/locality, rent, facilities, and photos, but the app withholds the exact street address, landmark, pincode, owner name, phone number, and saved map coordinates. Sign in to view those details, open the listing map or directions, or contact the owner. City and locality remain visible.
  • Contact and sharing: calling or WhatsApping an owner requires sign-in. After you choose one of those actions, the owner's listed phone number and a prefilled message are handed to the selected phone app or WhatsApp. If you share a listing, its link and selected listing details are passed to your device's share option or copied to your clipboard.
  • Saved properties: saved-property and bookmark records support your saved listings feature.
  • Location services and navigation:
    • Owner addresses: owners enter the street/house details, landmark, locality, city, state, and pincode manually. An existing listing may retain previously saved map coordinates while its address remains unchanged; changing the address clears those coordinates. Retained coordinates may be used to display a listing on a map.
    • Address search: text search matches listing locality, city, and landmark. State, city, and locality filters match the corresponding listing address details.
    • Listing maps and directions: opening a listing's map or directions may send its address or saved map coordinates to Google Maps.
  • Voice search: if you choose voice search, the browser/device Web Speech API converts speech to search text. Microphone access is requested only when you start the feature. Depending on your browser and device, audio may be processed by its speech-recognition provider. Where Is My Ghar does not store voice audio on its backend; the provider's privacy practices apply.
  • Preferences: language, theme, selected user mode, onboarding status, and app version may be saved on your device. Some preferences, such as language and user mode, may also be linked to your account.
  • Browser and app storage: local storage can keep your sign-in session, preferences, up to 30 previously used owner addresses, and an unfinished listing form with selected photo previews. The app treats that draft as expired after 10 minutes. Session storage can keep your search text, filters, sort order, saved-only choice, view choice, and scroll position for the current browser session. The browser does not show a separate permission prompt for this storage. Successful in-app account deletion clears account-specific address history and listing drafts from this device. Language, theme, and other general device preferences may remain. Signing out clears your sign-in session.
  • Property reports: when you report a listing, we store the listing and account identifiers, selected reason, date, and—only for "Other"— the description you enter (up to 500 characters). Reports help prevent repeat submissions and apply listing moderation. One account can report a listing once. Five reports from different signed-in accounts hide the listing from public feeds; the listing is not automatically deleted. Report details are not displayed publicly, and the owner sees only a status notice in the dashboard.

Permissions and your choices

  • Opening a listing's map or directions may send its address or saved map coordinates to Google Maps.
  • Voice search is optional and uses microphone permission. Depending on your app, browser, and device, speech audio may be processed by its speech-recognition service under that provider's privacy practices. You can use text search instead and manage microphone access through your app, browser, or device settings.
  • Property photo uploads are optional. Photos you choose to upload are stored in Supabase with the listing for guest property previews.
  • Calling or messaging an owner is optional. If you choose a call or WhatsApp action, the selected phone app or service handles that interaction under its own privacy practices.
  • You can remove saved properties in the app or request deletion of your account and associated data using the process on our Account Deletion page.

Services that process information

  • Supabase provides account authentication, session handling, and backend database/storage services for profiles, listings, saved properties, reports, and uploaded photos.
  • The current owner listing flow does not use a geocoding provider to fill address fields. Google Maps may receive a listing address or previously saved coordinates when you open its map or directions.
  • Unsplash provides fallback image assets used by the app.
  • Google Fonts supplies font assets requested by the app or website when it loads.
  • Your browser/device speech-recognition service may receive and process voice-search audio as described above; its privacy practices depend on the provider.
  • WhatsApp, your phone app, and your device's share feature are involved only when you choose to contact support or an owner, or share a listing. The selected app or service handles what you send under its own privacy practices.

These services process information under their own privacy notices and retention practices. Account deletion removes account data from Where Is My Ghar's Supabase backend; independent data processed by third-party services is handled under those services' own notices and retention practices.

Retention and account deletion

While your account is active, we keep the profile and listing information needed to run your account and show your listings. After you confirm in-app deletion, the app permanently deletes your account and login identity, profile, listings, uploaded listing photos, saved-property records, and account-owned reports, then signs you out. Account-specific address history and listing drafts on this device are also cleared. If you return later, sign up with the same phone number to create a new account. Deleted data cannot be restored.

If you cannot access the app, you can send an account-deletion request with your registered name and phone number by email

supportwhereismyghar@gmail.com

The support team manually reviews and processes email requests within 7 to 10 business days.

Account deletion removes account data from Where Is My Ghar's Supabase backend. Independent data processed by third-party services is handled under their own privacy notices and retention practices. See the Account Deletion page for instructions.

General device preferences, such as language and theme, may remain after account deletion. Account-specific address history and unfinished listing drafts are cleared by a successful in-app deletion. You can clear other saved website or app data through your browser or device settings.

Security

The publisher confirms that data is transmitted over HTTPS/TLS and backend data access is controlled using Supabase Row Level Security (RLS) policies. No additional security guarantees are made in this policy.
