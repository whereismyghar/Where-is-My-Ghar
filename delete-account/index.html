Account and data controls

Delete your account

Where Is My Ghar · 30-day deletion period

You can start account deletion in the app or by email. Both methods enter the account into the same 30-day grace period.

Delete from the app

  1. Sign in to your Where Is My Ghar account.
  2. Open Profile and choose Delete Account.
  3. Review the confirmation and confirm the request.

The account is deactivated during the 30-day grace period. To cancel deletion, sign back in to the account before the 30 days expire.

Request deletion by email

If you cannot use the app, email your account-deletion request to supportwhereismyghar@gmail.com. The publisher confirms that email requests are queued directly into the same 30-day deletion process without manual verification. Sign back in within the 30-day period to cancel.

Email a deletion request

Do not email your password or a one-time password (OTP).

What is deleted and when

At the end of the 30-day grace period, the publisher confirms that all account data is permanently deleted from the Where Is My Ghar Supabase backend. This includes account/profile data, property listings, uploaded listing photos, and saved property/bookmark records. The publisher confirms no post-deletion backend data or log exceptions are retained.

Account deletion removes account data from Where Is My Ghar's Supabase backend. Independent data processed by third-party services is handled under their own privacy notices and retention practices.

Need help?

For support or questions about a deletion request, contact supportwhereismyghar@gmail.com. See our Privacy Policy for information about data use, permissions, and third-party services.
