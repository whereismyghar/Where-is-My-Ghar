Account and data controls

Delete your account

Updated: November 20, 2026 · Website, app, and email options

You can permanently delete your account data from the app. If you cannot access the app, email us and the support team will review your request.

1. Request deletion in the app

  1. Go to Profile > Delete Account.
  2. Review the warning and confirm permanent deletion.

After confirmation, the app starts removing your profile, property listings, saved properties, reports, and uploaded listing photos. Once cleanup completes, After cleanup is complete, you'll be signed out. If you choose to use the app again later, you can sign in with the same phone number and set up a new profile. Your deleted profile and related app data won't be restored. This action cannot be undone.

2. Request deletion by email

If you cannot access the app, email your account-deletion request to

supportwhereismyghar@gmail.com

Include your registered name and phone number so the support team can review the request. The support team manually reviews and processes email deletion requests within 7 to 10 business days.

Email a deletion request

Do not email your password or a one-time password (OTP).

What is deleted and when

A successful in-app deletion removes the profile, property listings, uploaded listing photos, saved-property records, and account-owned reports. The After cleanup is complete, you'll be signed out. If you choose to return later, you can sign in with the same phone number and set up a new profile; your deleted profile and related app data won't be restored.

Account deletion removes account data from Where Is My Ghar's Supabase backend. Independent data processed by third-party services is handled under their own privacy notices and retention practices.

Some information is kept only on your device, such as language and theme preferences, saved address suggestions, and an unfinished listing draft. These local copies are not removed by deleting your online account. Signing out clears your sign-in session; you can clear other saved website or app data through your browser or device settings. An unfinished listing draft is treated as expired after 10 minutes.
