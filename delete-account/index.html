Account and data controls

Delete your account

Where Is My Ghar · In-app and email options

Choose the in-app process or, if you cannot access the app, request deletion by email. These methods have separate processing timelines.

1. Delete from the app (In-App Deletion)

  1. Go to Profile > Delete Account.
  2. Review and confirm the deletion request.

The account enters an automated 30-day grace period. Signing back in during this period automatically cancels the deletion request.

Once the 30 days expire, all user profile data, property listings, uploaded photos, and saved bookmarks are automatically and permanently deleted from the backend.

2. Request deletion by email

If you cannot access the app, email your account-deletion request to supportwhereismyghar@gmail.com. Include your registered name and phone number so the support team can review the request. The support team manually reviews and processes email deletion requests within 7 to 10 business days. This is separate from the in-app 30-day grace period.

Email a deletion request

Do not email your password or a one-time password (OTP).

What is deleted and when

For in-app requests, after the 30-day grace period all user profile data, property listings, uploaded photos, and saved bookmarks are automatically and permanently deleted from the Where Is My Ghar backend.

Account deletion removes account data from Where Is My Ghar's Supabase backend. Independent data processed by third-party services is handled under their own privacy notices and retention practices.

Need help?

For support or questions about a deletion request, contact supportwhereismyghar@gmail.com. See our Privacy Policy for information about data use, permissions, and third-party services.
